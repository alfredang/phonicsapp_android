# Changelog

All notable changes to the Phonics Android app are documented here.

## [1.0] — 2026-07-06 · versionCode 1

### Added
- Full phonics curriculum: consonants, short & long vowels, digraphs, blends, vowel teams, diphthongs, r-controlled vowels, and soft c/g — every sound with IPA, an articulation cue, and example words.
- One-tap voice-over in British (en-GB) or American (en-US) accent, fully offline.
- Real human word recordings (Wiktionary / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA) bundled for offline playback, with TTS fallback.
- Practice sessions: Intonation & Emotion (8 emotion presets), minimal pairs, tongue twisters, short messages, and sentence drills.
- Rules tab covering irregular spellings: silent letters, 'ough', soft c/g, i-before-e, sight words, the schwa, and doubling/drop-e.
- Settings: accent, female/male voice, and speaking-speed slider (persisted).
- Feedback via WhatsApp and About tab with audio attribution.
